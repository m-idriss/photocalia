# PhotoCalia launch plan

Status: publication-ready working kit, 7 August 2026. Nothing in this folder authorizes account creation or external publication.

## Positioning

PhotoCalia turns schedule images and PDFs into editable calendar events and an ICS file. The differentiator is the complete review workflow: upload, inspect every proposed event, correct it, then export to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook or another ICS-compatible app.

Primary audiences:

1. parents and caregivers handling school/activity schedules;
2. students importing timetables and exam dates;
3. clubs and small teams receiving schedules as PDF or image;
4. productivity users who receive appointment cards, flyers and screenshots.

## Launch sequence

1. Confirm the production conversion, privacy, pricing, support and mobile flows.
2. Record the 2–3 minute demo from the synthetic golden fixtures.
3. Create/claim LinkedIn and X profiles with owner-controlled credentials.
4. Build a Product Hunt draft from `PRODUCT_HUNT.md`, including gallery and demo.
5. Publish Product Hunt when the maker can answer comments throughout the day. Ask contacts to visit and discuss, never directly ask for upvotes.
6. Publish the LinkedIn launch post and X announcement with tagged campaign URLs.
7. Submit AlternativeTo and directories one at a time; record the canonical listing URL.
8. Participate in relevant Reddit communities before sharing. Re-check each community's current rules and disclose the maker relationship.
9. Publish the YouTube demo and embed/link it from launch profiles.
10. Review search visibility, qualified visits, conversion starts and completed exports after 2, 7 and 30 days.

## Human-controlled items

- account ownership, recovery email, password and 2FA;
- final screenshots/video showing production behavior;
- acceptance of each platform's current terms;
- external publication, comments and moderator contact;
- paid promotion or directory fees;
- user testimonials and review requests.

Product Hunt currently requires a personal account to post and says makers can post their own product. Its guidance prohibits directly asking people to upvote. LinkedIn requires a real personal account with authority to create the Page. Reddit prohibits repetitive unsolicited promotion and defers to community-specific rules.

## Launch gate

Do not publish until:

- all links use `https://www.photocalia.com` with the correct UTM set;
- claims match the current plan/provider/privacy behavior;
- no customer or private calendar appears in assets;
- the maker can respond to support and privacy requests;
- analytics consent and measurement are verified;
- every account is in the owner's password manager with 2FA and recovery documented.
