## Maker first comment

Hi Product Hunt — I built PhotoCalia because important dates still arrive in the least calendar-friendly formats: a school timetable on paper, a club schedule in a PDF, an appointment card, or a screenshot buried in a chat.

PhotoCalia gives those documents a practical path into a calendar. You upload an image or PDF, review the events proposed by multimodal AI, correct anything that needs attention, and download a standard ICS file. The review step is deliberate: dates and times matter too much to hide uncertainty behind a one-click promise.

The current version supports English and French pages, multi-file conversion, editable events, and exports compatible with major calendar applications. Google sign-in includes 3 free conversions per month.

I would especially value feedback on two questions:

1. Which schedule format do you still enter manually most often?
2. What would make you trust a document-to-calendar workflow enough to use it regularly?

I’ll be here to answer questions and share what I learn. Thanks for taking a look.
