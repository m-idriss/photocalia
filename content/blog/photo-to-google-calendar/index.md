Ever snapped a photo of a flyer, appointment card, or school schedule and wished it would just appear in your Google Calendar? With PhotoCalia, it can — in about 30 seconds. Here's exactly how.

## Why Convert Photos to Calendar Events?

We encounter calendar-worthy information everywhere: doctor appointment cards, concert posters, school newsletters, gym schedules, conference agendas. But manually typing each event into Google Calendar is tedious and error-prone. You might get the date wrong, forget the location, or simply never get around to it.

AI-powered photo-to-calendar conversion eliminates this friction entirely. You take a photo, the AI reads it, and your events are ready to save. No typing, no mistakes, no forgotten appointments.

## Step-by-Step: Photo to Google Calendar

### Step 1: Take a Photo or Screenshot

Use your phone camera to photograph any document containing event information — appointment cards, flyers, posters, schedules, or even WhatsApp messages with dates. You can also take a screenshot of a digital document. PhotoCalia supports JPG, PNG, and PDF formats.

### Step 2: Upload to PhotoCalia

Visit photocalia.com and sign in with your Google account (takes 2 seconds). Then drag and drop your image into the converter, or tap 'Browse' to select it from your device. You can upload multiple images at once for batch processing.

### Step 3: Review and Save Your Events

The AI instantly analyzes your image using GPT-4 Vision technology. It extracts event titles, dates, times, and locations. Review the results in a clean preview — edit anything that needs adjusting — then download your ICS file and import it into Google Calendar. Done!

## Tips for Best Results

- Use good lighting and avoid blurry photos — clear text gives the most accurate results

- Crop your image to focus on the event details (remove unnecessary background)

- For multi-page schedules, upload each page as a separate image for better accuracy

- Always review the extracted events before saving — AI is very accurate but a quick check ensures perfection

## What Can You Convert?

PhotoCalia works with virtually any image containing date and time information: doctor appointment cards, school event flyers, concert and festival posters, gym and yoga timetables, conference agendas, travel itineraries, delivery notifications, subscription renewal reminders, and more. If it has a date, PhotoCalia can extract it.
